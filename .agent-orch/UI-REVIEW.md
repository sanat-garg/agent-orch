# Mobile HIG review (task #178, 2026-09-26)

This review covers goal 6 of BRIEF.md: saved to the iPhone home screen, agent-orch should feel native.

**Setup.** I ran a test server on port 3977 with `CW_NO_ORCHESTRATOR=1` and a temp data dir. It was seeded with a login, a chat with messages and an orchestrator project (shop-app). The project has tasks that are done, failed, running, running after a move from Opus to GPT-5.5, and queued. Shots were taken with playwright-core at 390×844 CSS px, deviceScaleFactor 3, as an iPhone touch device. They are saved in `.agent-orch/shots/ui-review-*.png`.

The keyboard shot uses a 390×508 viewport, which is roughly the height left above the iOS keyboard. Touch-target sizes and font sizes were measured from the DOM. Contrast ratios were computed from the CSS tokens.

**Limits of this setup.** Chromium reports `env(safe-area-inset-*)` as 0. `ui-review-safe-area-sim.png` shows the layout with iPhone standalone insets injected by hand (top 59px, bottom 34px). The Connections shot shows this VM's real CLI sign-ins, because the test server reads the real `$HOME`.

Guidelines cited are the apple-design skill references, as `file › section`.

## What already looks right
- **Bottom sheets.** Queue (`ui-review-task-queue.png`) and While you were away (`ui-review-away.png`) are proper bottom sheets. They have a grabber, a large title, a close button, a dimmed backdrop and a scrolling body. Their `.aw-body` padding respects the bottom safe area (`sheets.md › Best practices`).
- **Composer text size.** The composer textarea is 16px, so iOS does not zoom on focus. The viewport has `viewport-fit=cover`, and the composer, drawer body, sidebar and toasts all pad with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
- **Login** (`ui-review-login.png`). One clear primary action, a 56pt password field with a reveal toggle, and a full-width Sign in button. Nothing clips.
- **Task drawer** (`ui-review-task-drawer.png`). It answers "which model, was it moved, what happens at a limit" in plain words: `GPT-5.5 · moved from Opus 5.5 (limit until 12:13 PM)`, an ordered fallback list marked "on it now", and a move log. This meets BRIEF goal 8.
- **Toasts** (`ui-review-toast.png`). On phones they sit at the top inside the safe area, with an accent edge, an action button (Undo) and a close button. They read like an iOS banner.
- **Dark mode tokens.** Dark-mode tokens exist (`prefers-color-scheme: dark`). The system font stack is used throughout (`typography.md`).
- **Overflow.** No modal overflows sideways at 375 or 390 wide (already guarded by test/ui-away.test.mjs).

## Round 2: what I checked and found fine
- **Sheets.** Settings, Stats, Skills & tools, Browser and a machine's detail are bottom sheets with a grabber, a large title, a close button and a dimmed backdrop. They run edge to edge at 390 px. No screen overflows sideways (`scrollWidth ≤ innerWidth` everywhere, and no clipped element).
- **Settings.** The auto-restart and sound toggles are real switches in labelled rows. Selects, Test/Upload and the direction presets are 44pt on touch. The long "Ask me before" hint wraps rather than truncating. With the keyboard up (`ui-review2-settings-keyboard-kb.png`), the header and the focused field stay visible.
- **Safe areas.** The full-screen Machines and Add machine modals pad the top inset (`ui-review2-machines-safe.png`, `ui-review2-add-machine-safe.png`). The Skills & tools editor's Cancel/Add bar pads the bottom inset (`ui-review2-extensions-editor-safe.png`). The live view pads all four insets (browser.css ≤800px block), and Files pads its path bar and Quick Look.
- **Machines.** Each card's Rename/Drain/Disable/Remove buttons are 44pt in an even row, with Remove in the danger colour. The cluster diagram and cards fit 390 px. Status is shown with words ("Online", "Offline · never connected"), not colour alone.
- **Approvals.** A held action shows the exact action, the page URL, the screenshot and the auto-deny time. Its buttons are 44pt. Deny asks why in a 16px textarea, so iOS doesn't zoom.
- **Files.** On touch, one tap opens Quick Look (files.js). Quick Look is full screen with 44pt icon buttons. List rows are 56pt on phones, with size and date under the name, as in Files on iOS.
- **Contrast.** In both themes the text tokens used on these screens pass. Light: `--muted` 5.35:1 and `--faint` 4.55:1 on `--bg`; the OUTBOUND `--warn` tag is 4.84:1; filled buttons use `--accent-strong`. Dark: `--muted` on `--raised` is 6.16:1. Stats cards, the hero number and the agent bars read well in dark (`ui-review2-stats-dark.png`).
- **Stats overview.** It answers "what did I get" first: the hero number ("11 tasks shipped") and a one-line summary, then 2-column cards. The Agents tab's per-model grid fits at 390 px.
